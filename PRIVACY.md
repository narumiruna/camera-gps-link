# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Camera GPS Link sends location information from an iPhone directly to a compatible camera over Bluetooth.
This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Data Collection

Camera GPS Link does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer.
The app does not use developer-operated servers, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party SDKs.
No account is required.

## Location Data

Camera GPS Link accesses the iPhone's location only after the user grants permission.
The app uses this location to provide geotagging information to a compatible camera selected by the user.
Location information is transmitted directly from the iPhone to the camera over Bluetooth and is not sent to the developer.
If the user enables background operation, the app may access location while running in the background, subject to iOS permissions and system scheduling.

## Bluetooth and Local Storage

Camera GPS Link uses Bluetooth to discover, remember, connect to, and send location updates to a compatible camera.
The app stores its settings and the identifier of the remembered camera locally on the iPhone using Apple system storage.
These values are not uploaded to the developer.

## Diagnostics

The app maintains a bounded diagnostic log in memory to help troubleshoot camera and location status.
Diagnostic information may include recent coordinates, camera connection state, and update times.
The app does not upload diagnostic information automatically.
Diagnostic information leaves the app only when the user explicitly copies or shares it.
Users should review and redact sensitive coordinates before sharing a diagnostic log.

## Retention and Deletion

App settings and the remembered camera identifier remain on the iPhone until the app updates them or the user uninstalls the app.
Diagnostic logs are held only in app memory and are not retained after the app process ends.
The app does not maintain a developer-accessible copy of location information.
A connected camera may cache location information or add it to newly captured photos according to the camera's own behavior.

## Third Parties

Camera GPS Link does not share data with advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers.
Information stored by or written to a connected camera is governed by the camera owner's practices and the camera manufacturer's policies.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the [Camera GPS Link support repository](https://github.com/narumiruna/sony-geotag/issues).
Please do not include precise coordinates or other sensitive information in a public issue.
